About Me

Security engineer, platform builder, problem-solver

I hold a Master's in Cybersecurity & Information Assurance and bring 15 years across IT and security — from infrastructure and identity to secure software design and AI development.

As founder of UNIFOSEC, I designed, built, and shipped ADATT, a commercial identity-security automation platform used by IT teams and MSPs to safely offboard users across Active Directory and Microsoft 365. I own the full secure SDLC — threat modeling, least-privilege architecture, secrets handling, audit logging, and shipping.

Today I focus on application security at scale: secure-by-design practices, CI/CD security guardrails, and applying AI/LLMs to automate security workflows and reduce manual toil.
